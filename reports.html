Report Notes

  • Only paid orders count as collected sales.
  • Pending payments are displayed separately and are not included in paid sales.
  • Cancelled orders are excluded.
  • Inventory purchases are not treated as immediate expenses.
  • Ingredient cost is estimated from recorded recipes and current ingredient prices.
  • Missing recipes or ingredients can cause ingredient costs to be understated.
  • Estimated profit is not verified final profit until recipe completeness and stock-cost calculations have been checked.

This report is read-only. It does not add, edit, or delete database records.
